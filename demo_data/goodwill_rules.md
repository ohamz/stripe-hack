# Goodwill rules

Written by Maya Chen. Each rule has an ID, who it applies to, and the rule in plain English.

## G1
Applies to: all clients

Small favours that take under 20 minutes are free, up to 2 per client per calendar month. After that, they're billed normally.

## G2
Applies to: acme

Typo fixes for Acme are always free and don't count towards the favour allowance.
