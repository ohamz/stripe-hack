# Design Services Agreement

Between **Maya Chen Design** (the Designer) and **Bright Bakery** (the Client).

Signed 1 September 2026.

## 1. Parties and timeline

- 1.1 The Designer will deliver the designs by 15 September 2026 (the Delivery Date).

## 2. Scope

- 2.1 The Designer will design two (2) deliverables: a menu (A4, 2 pages) and window signage (1 poster).
- 2.2 Each deliverable is supplied as a print-ready PDF.
- 2.3 Fixes to errors in delivered designs are included for 30 days after the Delivery Date.
- 2.4 Anything not listed in 2.1 to 2.3, including additional print items, digital and social media assets, is out of scope and billed under clause 5.

## 3. Revisions

- 3.1 The Client sends feedback by email.
- 3.2 Up to two (2) revision rounds per deliverable are included.
- 3.3 Additional revision rounds are billed under clause 5.

## 4. Meetings

- 4.1 One (1) meeting per calendar month, up to 30 minutes, is included.
- 4.2 Additional meetings, and time beyond 30 minutes in any meeting, are billed under clause 5.

## 5. Fees and invoicing

- 5.1 The fixed fee for the scope in clause 2 is £900 (paid separately, not handled by Tab).
- 5.2 Out-of-scope work is charged at £50 per hour.
- 5.3 Time is billed in 15-minute increments, rounded up per task.
- 5.4 Out-of-scope work is invoiced on the 1st of each month for the previous month. Payment is due within 14 days.
